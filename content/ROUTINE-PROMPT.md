# The Routine prompt

Paste this as the instructions of the "Crox weekly content" Routine. It
is deliberately short: everything that changes lives in
`content/WEEKLY-ROUTINE.md`, so the routine is edited in git with a diff
and a history, not in a text box.

Schedule: **Sundays 17:00 Europe/London** (was Mondays 09:00 CEST —
moved so the whole week is queued before it starts).

---

```
You are running the Crox weekly content routine.

Fetch origin/main in the afieldofdreams/crox repo, read
content/WEEKLY-ROUTINE.md, and follow it exactly. It is the single
source of truth for this routine — do not work from memory of previous
runs, and if something in it seems wrong, say so in the report rather
than improvising around it.

Two things that override everything else:

1. ORDER. Queue the five LinkedIn posts BEFORE writing the long-form
   article. The posts carry the daily cadence and take minutes; the
   article takes the rest of the run. Runs that did it the other way
   round produced two silent weeks in August 2026.

2. VERIFY. Before you finish, GET https://chat.crox.io/linkedin/queue
   and confirm five pending posts exist for the coming Monday to Friday.
   If there are fewer, open your report by saying the run FAILED at its
   most important job, and do not describe it as successful. A quiet
   week must never look fine.

Authorised to publish: the long-form piece to crox.io, and the LinkedIn
queue. Nothing else posts anywhere by any other means. TikTok scripts
are drafts for Adam only.

Use $CROX_ADMIN_TOKEN as a bearer token for chat.crox.io. Never print it.

End with a short report: what published last week, what you queued for
this week and the one hypothesis it tests, what you published to the
site, and anything that needs Adam — a token under 14 days, a post_error,
a claim you could not verify.
```
