# Fred — the back-to-school launch window

Written 2026-08-21. Six Reels are banked (rendered from
`scripts/demos/`, one per scenario) and UK terms start the first week
of September — the fortnight when school admin content is at peak
relevance and peak search. So the six don't drip out one a week: they
front-load through the window, then the normal cadence
(`FRED-INSTAGRAM-PLAN.md`) takes over.

Posting is native from the phone: **no music on any of these** (audio
makes Instagram render full-screen and crop), evening slots per the
plan. Captions below are ready to paste — keywords are in the text
because that's what Instagram search reads; no hashtag spam.
